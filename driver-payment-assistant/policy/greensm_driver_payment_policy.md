# GreenSM Driver Payment Policy

**Effective date:** January 1, 2026  
**Applies to:** Active GreenSM driver-partners in the United States  
**Document owner:** GreenSM Driver Finance  
**Version:** 2026.1

This document is the official ground truth for how GreenSM pays drivers. Support agents must answer payment questions using only this policy.

---

## 1. Purpose and scope

GreenSM pays drivers for completed trips, eligible tips, and qualifying incentives. This policy covers:

- Weekly automatic payouts
- Instant Cash-Out
- Minimum balances and thresholds
- Payment methods
- Holiday and bank-delay handling
- Tips, promotions, and incentives
- Fare adjustments, cancellations, and disputed fares
- Tax reporting at a high level
- When to escalate to a human Driver Pay Specialist

This policy does **not** cover rider billing, vehicle leasing, or insurance claims.

---

## 2. Earnings definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Trip earnings** | Base fare + time + distance + surge/prime-time, minus applicable GreenSM service fees, as shown in the Driver app after the trip |
| **Tips** | Rider tips attributed to a trip; paid in full to the driver (GreenSM does not take a cut of tips) |
| **Incentives** | Quest bonuses, streak bonuses, guaranteed earnings top-ups, and other promotions that meet stated terms |
| **Available balance** | Funds cleared and ready for weekly payout or Instant Cash-Out |
| **Pending balance** | Funds not yet cleared (for example, under review or within the post-trip clearance window) |
| **Held balance** | Funds temporarily withheld due to a dispute, fare adjustment review, or compliance hold |

**Clearance window:** Trip earnings and tips typically move from Pending to Available within **2 hours** after trip completion. Incentives post according to the promotion’s own schedule (often within 24 hours after the promotion period ends).

## 3. Weekly payout schedule

GreenSM’s standard pay cycle is **weekly**.

| Item | Rule |
|------|------|
| **Earnings week** | Monday 00:00 local driver timezone through Sunday 23:59 local driver timezone |
| **Payout processing day** | Every **Monday** GreenSM initiates ACH transfer of the Available balance from the prior earnings week |
| **Expected bank deposit** | Typically **Wednesday** of the same calendar week (1–3 business days after Monday initiation) |
| **Minimum for weekly payout** | **$1.00** Available balance. Balances under $1.00 roll forward to the next week |
| **Auto-payout** | Enabled by default for all drivers with a verified bank account on file |

### 3.1 What is included in the weekly payout

- Available trip earnings from the completed earnings week
- Available tips from that week
- Available incentives that cleared into Available before Monday processing
- Any Instant Cash-Out fees already deducted are reflected in the remaining Available balance (they are not refunded via weekly payout)

### 3.2 What is not included

- Pending balance still in the clearance window
- Held balance under dispute or review
- Amounts already withdrawn via Instant Cash-Out

### 3.3 Example

Driver completes trips Monday–Sunday. On the following Monday, GreenSM initiates payout of Available balance. The driver should see the deposit in their bank account by Wednesday in most cases, subject to their bank’s posting times.

## 4. Instant Cash-Out

Drivers may move Available balance to an eligible debit card before the weekly payout.

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Eligibility** | Active driver account in good standing; verified eligible debit card linked; no open Instant Cash-Out fraud hold |
| **Minimum per cash-out** | **$5.00** |
| **Maximum per cash-out** | **$500.00** |
| **Daily cash-out limit** | **$1,000.00** total Instant Cash-Out per calendar day (local driver timezone) |
| **Fee** | **$1.99** flat fee per Instant Cash-Out, deducted from Available balance at request time |
| **Speed** | Usually arrives within **a few minutes**; may take up to **30 minutes** during peak load |
| **Cutoff** | Instant Cash-Out is available **24/7**, including weekends and most holidays |
| **Failed cash-out** | If the transfer fails, funds (including the fee) are returned to Available balance within **1 business day**; the driver may retry |

Instant Cash-Out does **not** include Pending or Held balance. Drivers cannot Instant Cash-Out tips or trip earnings that have not cleared to Available.

---

## 5. Minimum thresholds (summary)

| Action | Minimum |
|--------|---------|
| Weekly automatic payout | **$1.00** Available |
| Instant Cash-Out | **$5.00** Available (plus enough to cover the $1.99 fee) |

If Available balance is $5.00 and the driver requests Instant Cash-Out, GreenSM deducts $1.99 and sends $3.01 to the debit card. If Available is under $6.99, the driver may still cash out $5.00 only when Available ≥ $5.00 **and** Available ≥ cash-out amount + $1.99. In practice, the app requires Available ≥ requested amount + $1.99.

## 6. Payment methods

### 6.1 Bank account (ACH) — required for weekly payout

- Drivers must link a **U.S. checking or savings account** in their name (or joint account where they are a listed owner).
- Bank accounts are verified via micro-deposits or instant verification partner.
- Weekly payouts are sent **only** by ACH to the primary verified bank account.
- Changing the primary bank account: request in the Driver app → **Wallet → Payment methods**. Changes submitted before **Sunday 18:00 local time** apply to the upcoming Monday payout; later changes apply to the following week’s payout.

### 6.2 Debit card — Instant Cash-Out only

- Must be a U.S.-issued Visa or Mastercard debit card eligible for push-to-card transfers.
- Prepaid cards, gift cards, and credit cards are **not** supported.
- Card must be in the driver’s name.
- Drivers may store up to **two** debit cards; Instant Cash-Out uses the card marked as default.

### 6.3 Unsupported methods

GreenSM does not pay drivers by check, wire, PayPal, Venmo, Cash App, cryptocurrency, or cash pickup.

## 7. Holidays and bank delays

| Situation | What happens |
|-----------|----------------|
| **U.S. federal bank holiday falling on Monday** | Weekly payout initiation shifts to the **next banking business day**. Deposit may arrive **Thursday** instead of Wednesday |
| **Bank holiday mid-week** | ACH may post one extra business day late; Instant Cash-Out (debit card) is usually unaffected |
| **Driver’s bank internal hold** | GreenSM’s transfer may show as sent while the bank delays posting 1–2 extra days. GreenSM cannot force the bank to release funds early |
| **GreenSM platform holidays** | Driver Pay Support chat may have reduced hours; Instant Cash-Out and automated weekly processing continue unless a separate outage notice is posted in the app |

If a weekly payout was initiated and has not appeared after **5 business days**, the driver should escalate (see Section 11).

## 8. Tips, incentives, and statements

### 8.1 Tips

- 100% of rider tips go to the driver.
- Tips appear on the trip receipt in the Driver app and on the weekly earnings statement under **Tips**.
- Tips follow the same clearance window as trip earnings (typically Available within 2 hours).

### 8.2 Incentives and promotions

- Quest, streak, and guarantee bonuses credit only when **all stated promotion terms** are met.
- Incentive credits post as a separate line item labeled with the promotion name.
- If a promotion is under review (for example, suspected abuse), related incentive amounts may remain **Pending** or move to **Held** until review completes (typically within **7 calendar days**).

### 8.3 Earnings statements

- Weekly statements are available in **Wallet → Statements** by Tuesday each week for the prior earnings week.
- Statements show trip earnings, tips, incentives, Instant Cash-Out withdrawals, Instant Cash-Out fees, adjustments, and ending Available balance.

## 9. Fare adjustments, cancellations, and disputed fares

### 9.1 Rider cancellations

| Timing | Driver payment |
|--------|----------------|
| Rider cancels before driver is en route | **$0** |
| Rider cancels after driver is en route but before arrival | Cancellation fee per local market schedule (shown in app; often a fixed amount) |
| Rider cancels after driver arrives and waits the required wait time | Cancellation fee + applicable wait-time pay per market rules |

### 9.2 Fare adjustments

- GreenSM may adjust a fare when the mapped route differs materially from the actual trip, or when a rider or driver reports an issue.
- Downward adjustments reduce Available (or Pending) balance for that trip.
- Upward adjustments credit Available balance, usually within **24 hours** of approval.
- Drivers are notified in-app when a fare on a completed trip is adjusted by more than **$1.00**.

### 9.3 Disputed fares and holds

- A driver or rider may open a fare dispute within **14 days** of the trip.
- While under review, the disputed amount may be moved from Available/Pending to **Held**.
- Standard review time is **3–5 business days**; complex cases may take up to **10 business days**.
- If the dispute is resolved in the driver’s favor, Held funds return to Available.
- If resolved against the driver, Held funds are removed and the statement shows an adjustment line.

### 9.4 Negative balances

If adjustments and fees exceed Available balance, the account may show a **negative Available balance**. Future earnings first repay the negative balance before weekly payout or Instant Cash-Out is allowed.

## 10. Tax documents (high level)

- Drivers are independent contractors for tax purposes unless otherwise notified in writing.
- GreenSM issues **Form 1099-NEC** (or successor form) when legally required based on annual earnings thresholds set by the IRS.
- Tax summaries and downloadable tax forms appear in **Wallet → Tax info** when available (typically late January for the prior tax year).
- This policy does not provide tax advice. Drivers with tax questions should consult a tax professional or request a human Driver Pay Specialist for help locating documents—not for interpreting tax law.

## 11. Escalation to human Driver Pay Support

The voice agent (and automated support) should escalate to a human **Driver Pay Specialist** when:

1. A weekly payout was initiated and has not arrived after **5 business days**
2. An Instant Cash-Out failed and funds did not return to Available within **1 business day**
3. Available balance does not match the driver’s trip history and the agent cannot reconcile using this policy alone
4. The driver reports unauthorized payment method changes
5. A dispute has exceeded **10 business days** without resolution
6. The driver needs help with tax form access beyond “where to find it in the app”
7. The driver asks about account suspension, background checks, or non-payment topics outside this policy

**Escalation phrase for agents:**  
“I can only help with GreenSM’s published payment policy. For your specific account, I’ll connect you with a Driver Pay Specialist—or you can open Wallet → Help → Pay & wallet in the Driver app.”

## 12. Quick answers for common questions

**“When do I get paid?”**  
Standard pay is weekly. Earnings from Monday through Sunday are paid out starting the following Monday by ACH, and usually arrive in your bank by Wednesday. You can also Instant Cash-Out Available balance to a debit card for a $1.99 fee, usually within minutes.

**“What’s the Instant Cash-Out fee?”**  
$1.99 per cash-out. Minimum $5.00; max $500 per transfer; $1,000 daily limit.

**“Why is my money pending?”**  
Trip earnings and tips usually clear to Available within about 2 hours. Incentives and disputed amounts may take longer.

**“Do you take a cut of tips?”**  
No. Drivers receive 100% of rider tips.

**“What if Monday is a bank holiday?”**  
Weekly payout initiates on the next banking business day; the deposit may arrive a day later than usual (often Thursday).

## 13. Policy changes

GreenSM may update this policy with notice in the Driver app and by email to the address on file. Material changes to fees or payout timing will be announced at least **14 days** before they take effect, except where required sooner by law or to prevent fraud.
